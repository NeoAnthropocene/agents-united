# Claude Code 2.1.325: the factory's first real member, `subagent-devops-engineer` (pilot; 2026-11-18)

**These are observations of one installed build, not documentation.** The guides in `../guide/` cite the vendor's docs snapshots; where this file disagrees with a guide, the guide's authoring notes say so. Nothing here is a vendor promise, and it may change in any release.

After the maintainer parked the Codex stage ("Park Codex and finish the Claude bundles first."), the factory was to be used on a real Bundle for the first time. The pilot is the smallest real case: the Bundle `devops-engineering` has one member, `subagent-devops-engineer`, and its orchestrator and parent Bundle already have Claude-native files. Until now the factory had only produced small test artifacts.

**Who sat it.** The maintainer asked the lead of the session of 2026-11-18 (an AI session) to run the pilot while he sat other cases. The lead sat one interactive session through its terminal bridge. No human typed in it: the lead typed the starting prompt, the answers to the coordinator's questions and the `/author-contract` line. Where the coordinator's report says "you" or "your answers", that is the lead.

## What this shows and what it does not

Shown once: on a real member the factory's contract route and its realizer produce candidates that pass every deterministic caller check, and the coordinator asks instead of guessing when an input is missing or a check refuses.

Not shown: an independent review that passes its checker at the first attempt. In this pilot one evaluator result of four did. The stage review of 2026-11-17 already recorded that an evaluator's first-attempt citation compliance is not shown as fixed; here it cost journey 2 its review, and a second failure kind appeared (a result in prose). With one additional evaluator turn per journey, a member's realization can end unreviewed.

Two choices of the authoring fork are open and the maintainer's: the pair has no `messaging` capability (direct peer messaging is stated as an invariant), and the destructive-command guard is left to the native lane, which today does not enforce it (P9).

The candidates are in the workspace and not in the repository: `work\draft\pair\core\` (the pair) and `work\draft2\agent\hosts\claude\agents\devops-engineer.md` (the agent).
