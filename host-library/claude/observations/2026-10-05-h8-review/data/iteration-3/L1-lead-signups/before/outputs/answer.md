**Step 2: ask a few questions that change the plan.** These are the ones I'd put to the client:
1. **Deadline.** Which date is "end of quarter"? Today is 6 Oct, so it could be 31 Dec or a fiscal date. That sets whether we have about 12 weeks or about 4.
2. **Target.** How many signups, and from what baseline? What is the current monthly rate?
3. **Definition.** Is a signup a free account, a trial start or a paid conversion? An account that never sends an invoice may not count for them.
4. **Audience.** Who is it for: freelancers, small businesses or accountants? Which countries? That decides the regulatory angle (e-invoicing mandates) and the channels.
5. **Funnel.** Where does traffic come from today, and where do people drop off (visit, signup, first invoice sent)?
6. **Constraints.** What is the budget, who can ship site changes, and what can we measure (analytics access)?
