## What was established

- **The settings-level guard refuses a command it names when a teammate runs it (the question of H5).** `probe` made five `Bash` calls. #1 `echo git push --force` and #2 `echo x > .env.test` came back with `is_error` and `PreToolUse:Bash hook error: [node ${CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR}/.claude/hooks/agents-united-guard.js]: Blocked by agents-united guard: ...` (the helper prints a shortened message; the full text is in the record). #3 `echo git push --force-with-lease` printed `git push --force-with-lease`, #4 `echo x > .env.example` ran with no output and #5 `echo hello world` printed `hello world`. The controls ran and only the two named commands were refused: the guard does not over-match.
- **On disk:** `.env.test` does **not** exist; `.env.example` exists and contains `x`.
- **The settings-level hook command resolves for a teammate.** The command in the record is `node ${CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR}/.claude/hooks/agents-united-guard.js`, and the script ran (it is what refused the two commands).
- **The lead ran none of the commands** (its calls: `Agent`, `ToolSearch`, `SendMessage`), as the prompt asked.
- **Shutdown was structured and answered.** The lead sent `{"type":"shutdown_request",...}` at 13:23:43 and `probe` answered with a `shutdown_response` (`approve: true`) at 13:23:46, read from the probe's own call. The helper's line "shutdown responses received by the lead: 0" under-reports it (the lead's record did not carry the reply): a helper limitation, fixed in #147.
- **Gate: pass** (the protocol's own words): commands 1 and 2 refused by the hook, `H5 seen` with exactly those two entries, no `.env.test`, commands 3, 4 and 5 ran.
