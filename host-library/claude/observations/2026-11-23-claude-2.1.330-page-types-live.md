## Findings, slips and deviations

- **The plan answer withdrew the publish go-ahead (design lead, P1 and P7).** The script's plan answer says it answers no question about publishing anything. Both times the lead had already been told "Publish Artifact" and then, after the plan answer, read the go-ahead as narrowed to local files and made no page, offering the publish in prose only. In the other three sessions no plan dialog came. The sitter followed the script as written and typed nothing to the prose offers. The lesson for a sitting script: a plan answer of "local work only" must not be given where a publish go-ahead has already been given, because the lead reads it as narrowing that go-ahead. The leads' reading is safe behaviour.
- **Sitter's slip, business P6.** A relayed lead question ("is the existing quality mechanic already one of the six starting staff?") stayed open for about 20 minutes because the sitter's wait views were clipped to the screen tail. It was answered by the keystrokes of the `/exit` that ended the session, which chose its first option, the one the script prescribes anyway. It does not touch publishing. A slip, not a spoil.
- After the sessions had ended the coordinating session sent three messages (answer the design lead's prose offers with "Yes, publish it."; type "Continue." once into the stalled business lead; do not restart a closed design session). All three arrived after the sessions had closed, so none was typed.
- Not done: no Slides page, no sharing, no sign-in, nothing deleted or pinned, no `/design consent`, `/design-login` or `/design-sync`.
