## Growth Strategy Playbook: PetPal launch (scratch exercise, provisional)

### Growth Brief (5 lines)
1. **ICP:** pet owners of 1-2 dogs or cats who travel 2+ times a year (owner side of the marketplace).
2. **Primary KPI:** booking-request rate = booking requests / unique hero visitors (single KPI; no baseline yet).
3. **Positioning angle:** trust over price. "Verified local sitters and a daily photo update while you are away."
4. **Bottleneck:** Acquisition-to-Activation (visit to first booking request). Provisional: no funnel data seen.
5. **UTM scheme:** `utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=<email|social|cpc|referral>&utm_campaign=petpal_launch&utm_content=<asset>`

### Checkable Headline Claim
"Every PetPal sitter is identity-checked before their first booking."
Defne must confirm the check exists and the wording is lawful before it ships. If unconfirmed, drop "identity-checked".

- CTA testId: `hero-primary-cta`

### Experiment Brief
- **Primary Metric:** booking-request rate (hero visit to request).

- **Control:** hero with headline and CTA only.
- **Variant:** same hero plus the headline claim and a sitter-verification line beside `hero-primary-cta`.
- **Run rule:** 2 weeks maximum; size with ab-test-setup once weekly traffic is known (under ~500 visitors a week, use qualitative review instead).

- No dark patterns; every claim must be true and checkable.
