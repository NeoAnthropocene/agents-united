## What Sitting T says

1. **The amended hook released both leads within three rounds of holds.** H10k2: 5 calls in 3 messages; G1: 3 calls in 3 messages; against 13 and 11 in Sitting S. Neither lead stopped, asked the user to reply, or said that the hook was broken. In both the message of round 1 was followed at once (the line as a text block with the repeated call, although the lead's reasoning held it): the sentence "even if you believe you wrote it already" did what the conditional one of Sitting S could not. The replay of the installed hook on each final transcript finds the line (exit 0): once in H10k2, twice in G1.
2. **The order of ADR 0051 was not followed in any of the four sessions of Sittings S and T.** The line and the introduction stand in the reasoning of message 2 each time, so a session shows red hook errors (three rounds here) and the lead never introduces itself. The order is a preference of the model's, not a rule of the host: only the hook is certain.
3. **The host's save lagged more than the lead's round trip.** In G1 the hook, at +27.7 s, still saw no text block of the lead although the line had been written at +21.7 s; the message of round 3 said so and the lead, as told, wrote the line again. That is the second line in G1: a duplicate that the design accepts (one duplicate for a dead session), and the cause of the third red error.
4. **The offer was reached with figures that agree with the checker** (12 of 15 items). The misses are lines the lead's text asks for and it left out: "no free tier" in an option, and the go-ahead with the ceiling asked (it took the choice as the go).
5. **The lead never saw the image tool, and neither did Jamileh.** The lead's `tools` did not name it, the session cut its tool pool to that list, and a subagent inherits the MCP tools of the main conversation. The two restarts and the `/mcp` reconnect of the maintainer could not have changed it. The image route had only ever been run with Jamileh as the main agent (H10d), where her own list is the pool, and the lead's check (`ToolSearch` for the tool) was written for a lead that holds nothing of the server. A suite now pins the rule for every coordinator.
6. **The lead told the user to expect an approval prompt that a local server does not have,** and **the first restart carried no `--model sonnet`, and neither restart set the task-list variable.** The first is in the lead's section and in the reference and is corrected; the second is the launcher's.
7. **The sitting cost 2.6494 USD in 2 prompts** and made no image.
