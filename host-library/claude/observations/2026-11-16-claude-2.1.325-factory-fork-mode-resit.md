Finding S4-A1 did not recur in this session. The destructive-operation guard inside `factory-realizer` is shown for `Bash` as well as for `PowerShell` (first sitting).
- **Not determinable from the record:** which of the two switches made the difference (the environment value or the `--settings` element), and which of the two variables (the first sitting already had `CLAUDE_CODE_EXPERIMENTAL_AGENT_TEAMS=0`, so `CLAUDE_CODE_FORK_SUBAGENT=0` is the one that changed, though the record cannot prove causation); whether a call without `run_in_background` would still go to the background (both calls passed `false`, and `M` line 25 still describes background as the default); the launcher's own environment and argv (only the printed PowerShell line is in the record); and whether the realizer's use of `Bash` despite `PowerShell` as its primary shell has any link to the foreground launch.
