## The three red errors

The maintainer reported them: "I encountered those warning below 3 times in the beginning", and pasted the third, "hold 3 ... The saved transcript has 0 text block(s) of yours". **The second and third were false.** The lead did what the first hold said: message 4 opened a text block with the line and repeated the call. The record shows the text entry written before the call and before the hold's result, in this order (`file-order-h10m.txt`): line 72 the text (+18.7 s), line 73 the call (+20.1 s), line 74 its error. But the hook, which reads the transcript from disk, saw no text block of the lead at +20.2 s or at +23.8 s, 5.1 s after the line was written. **The host saves its transcript late**, and its hooks guide says so of `transcript_path` ("may lag the in-memory conversation ... when a hook fires"); `PreToolUse` input carries no text. The line was in the file by +27.8 s.

Across the interactive sessions of Sittings P, T and U in which a lead wrote the line and was held afterwards (`lag-bounds.txt`) the line was missing from the file **2.7 s (H10k2 of Sitting T), 5.0 s (this session), 5.7 s (H10k2 of Sitting P) and 6.0 s (G1 of Sitting T)** after it was written, and present by 9.2 s (this session) and 10.4 s (G1 of Sitting T) at the latest the record allows. The lead followed every message of the hook; only the file was late. ADR 0053 adds a bounded wait for the file to the hook.

## What Sitting U says

1. **The fix of Sitting T works.** With the lead's `tools` naming the one tool, the lead's pool listed it and so did the pool of a teammate that was given the lead's MCP tools: 45 and 23 tools, one match each. The suite that pins every coordinator (`tests/native-claude-lead-pool.test.ts`) had said so; a session has now shown it.
2. **Three red hook errors, two of them false.** The first was the design (the lead had not written its line: it kept it in its reasoning, as in 5 of 5 interactive sessions of Sittings S, T and U); the second and third were the host's late save, not the lead's fault. A hold message cannot cure a file that is behind.
3. **The lead followed the messages of the hook exactly** (the line with the first repeat, the call alone with the second, the line again with the third) and ended with the line written twice, the duplicate that the design accepts.
4. **The relay was three sentences and the lead did not explain that it holds the tool for her.** Neither is a fault of the fix. The second is a pass-line item that the prompt did not ask for.
5. **The shutdown answer came as an object on her first call**, and the record of the lead ends before it processed it: a session that ended, not a refusal.
6. **The maintainer then asked for the welcome message of the Tier-2 orchestrators to be removed** and for the Operation Mode information to be the first message, "without the hook errors as we expect". ADR 0053 and the trials of `2026-10-13-claude-2.1.296-first-message-trials.md` are the answer.
