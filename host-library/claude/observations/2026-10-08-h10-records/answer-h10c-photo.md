I couldn't make the photo. This environment has no image generation, and I won't draw an illustration and call it a photo. What I did instead is build the 1:1 feed ad layout around a labelled photo slot, with a brief for getting the real image. I re-read both files after writing them. I haven't rendered the SVG or run a contrast checker; the contrast figures are my own calculation.

**What's in the layout**
- **Photo slot:** 1080×640, full bleed. There is no text on the photo, so any photo can drop in without a scrim. A dashed box marks where the faces of the sitter and dog should sit.
- **Text panel:** a cream panel below the photo holds the headline, the CTA button and a logo box. All of it sits inside the 108 px safe margin. The headline, CTA and logo are bracketed placeholders.
- **Colours:** I used a provisional palette of dark ink, cream and a terracotta CTA, because the project has no brand tokens or logo. Ink on cream is about 14:1 and white on the CTA is about 5.4:1. Replace the palette once you have real tokens.

**Options for getting the photo** (the brief lists four, with cost and trust trade-offs)
- **Real shoot (my recommendation):** a vetted sitter with their own dog in a real home, shot on a phone in window light. It fits "warm, natural" best and costs least. It needs a signed model and property release.
- **Licensed stock (stopgap):** check that the licence covers paid ads. It's weakest on trust if the ad promises real sitters.
- **AI-generated image:** make it outside this tool and get legal review first. Disclose it where the platform requires, and don't present it as a real sitter or customer.
- **Illustration:** I can draw this in SVG, but it isn't the photo you asked for.

The brief also has a shot list covering framing, light, a plain sofa, and keeping faces out of the crop edges.
