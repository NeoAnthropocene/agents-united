# Hero photo brief: sitter and dog on a sofa (provisional)

Status: no photography exists and this environment cannot render raster images, so no photo is produced here. `ad-feed-1x1-photo-slot.svg` is the layout with a labelled slot.

## Options to fill the slot (pick one)
| Option | Authentic? | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Real shoot: a vetted sitter, their own dog, a real home, phone is enough | Yes | Lowest real cost | Best fit for "warm, natural". Needs signed model and property release. |
| B. Licensed stock | Looks staged | Low | Check licence covers paid ads. Weakest on trust if the ad promises real sitters. |
| C. AI-generated image (generated outside this tool) | No | Low | Disclose where the platform requires it. Do not present as a real sitter or real customer. Legal review before use. |
| D. Illustration (SVG) in place of a photo | Honest | Design time | Can be done here, but it is not the photo you asked for. |

I recommend A for a first test, with B as a stopgap. Do not use a photo of people who are not sitters on your platform with wording that implies they are.

## Shot list (option A or B)
- Frame: 3:2 landscape or 4:5, shot wide enough to crop to 1080x640 (feed), 1080x1350 and 1080x1920.
- Subjects: sitter and dog at eye level, sitting close, dog relaxed, both faces in the centre 50% of the frame.
- Light: window daylight, side-lit, warm, no flash.
- Sofa and room: plain, uncluttered, a texture or two (throw, wool), no visible brands.
- Keep the lower 30% and the top 15% free of faces and important detail (crops and platform UI).
- Deliver 3 to 5 takes with different dog poses, for the variant test.
