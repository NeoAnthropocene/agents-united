# Claude Code 2.1.289: asking for missing inputs before a skill runs (Plan 035 M3, H8 follow-up, 2026-10-06)

**These are observations of one installed build, not documentation.** The guides in `../guide/` cite the vendor's docs snapshots; where this file disagrees with a guide, the guide's authoring notes say so. Nothing here is a vendor statement.

- **Build:** Claude Code `2.1.289`, Windows 11. The docs library is reviewed against `2.1.285`.
- **Account:** Claude Pro, extra usage off.
- **Question:** the maintainer's concern after H8 (the skills cost more than no skill on some prompts): should the orchestration agent, and the specialists, collect the data a skill needs before it runs, instead of paying for a provisional answer?
- **Method:** the H8 method, headless (`claude -p --agent <role> --model sonnet --effort medium --permission-mode auto --output-format json --max-budget-usd 0.8`). "Before" is `dev` plus #148 to #150, installed fresh with `agents add digital-agency -t claude --native --session-guard local -y`; "after" adds the two branches below, installed fresh the same way. Assertions were written before any run (`2026-10-05-h8-review/data/assertions-iteration-3.json`); every verdict has quoted evidence in `data/iteration-3/`, the answers are in `review-intake.html`, both in `2026-10-05-h8-review/`.

## What was established

- **The rule, version 1, cut a data-poor answer to about a third and broke a data-rich one.** "Inputs first" in the nine specialist bodies (check the brief before loading a skill or writing a file; if an input is missing, ask in a short first reply with a labelled provisional sketch, no file): the growth prompt with no numbers went from 3,683 to 1,119 output tokens (0.145 to 0.102 USD), the funnel prompt from four numbers and no page from 4,547 to 1,754 (0.168 to 0.109, and it no longer wrote a file). But on the control, the growth prompt that carries the full funnel counts and asks "what should we test first?", the role stopped to ask for price and plan model (which matter only for LTV), withheld the backlog and the briefs, and did not load the skill: 2 of 7 assertions against 3 of 7.
- **Version 2 defines "required" narrowly and passes the control.** An input is required only when the answer would otherwise rest on numbers or pages not seen; one that would only sharpen it goes under `Open items` and the artifact is delivered; the first reply also says what the numbers it has already show. The control then loaded the skill, delivered a condensed playbook (ICE backlog, three briefs, a schedule) and scored 3 of 7 again; the two data-poor prompts stayed short (1,169 and 1,553 output tokens, 0.087 and 0.107 USD, no file).
- **Pass counts on the data-poor prompts:** growth 5 of 7 before and after (the short reply gives up "instrumentation is experiment zero, owned by Deniz and Emre" and the scored ideas, and gains "no file and no brief"); funnel 5 of 6 before and 4 of 6 after (the short reply does not say that no flow was walked and does not flag the benchmark; version 2 does name the leak, version 1 called the stages "similar-sized levers"). The saving is output tokens and cost, not a higher pass rate.
- **The lead's change is small and its skill step was not exercised.** In `agency-brief-and-premises` the lead now lists the data the first slices need (from each skill's `Inputs` line) and asks the missing data in the same round, and the lead's body says so. On the "we need more signups" eval the lead scored 3 of 8 before and 4 of 8 after, and the after reply has an explicit "Funnel data" group and says "the specialists would be working from guesses". **Neither run loaded `agency-brief-and-premises`**, so only the lead's sentence acted; the difference is one assertion and it is the approaches, not the data list. Both leads asked six questions at once (the skill says one at a time) and neither lists the data with who can supply each.
- **A race in the test double surfaced twice in the full suite** (`test_run_scans`, one call logged instead of two, then invalid JSON): `run_scans.py` runs scans concurrently and `fake_semgrep.py` appended every call to one file, which Windows does not make atomic. It reproduces every time when forty processes spin to one instant and then call the fake five times each. Fixed in the fake (one file per process), not in the script.

## Not established

- Whether a specialist other than Ava and Kaan behaves the same: the rule is the same text in nine bodies and pinned there, measured on two.
- Whether the lead's brief skill, once it is loaded, asks for the data better than the lead's sentence alone.
- Whether the short reply is worth the user's extra turn: tokens and cost fall by 61 to 68 percent and 30 to 40 percent per run, but the full artifact now needs a second prompt.
- Every figure is one run per cell (the noise measured in H8 is about one assertion); the control's v2 run cost 27 percent more than its before run, which is within that variation but is one sample.
