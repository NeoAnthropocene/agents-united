# PetPal Launch Kit (pilot)
Scratch exercise: placeholder domain, no real client. Goal event: sitter search started. Audience (assumed): opted-in sign-ups, urban owners 25-45. No statistics, testimonials or urgency. "ID-checked" and "reviews" await Defne's review. Landing page CTA contract (once): testId `hero-primary-cta`, token `color.cta.primary`, label "Find a sitter near you".

## (a) 3-email drip
All emails: one CTA "Find a sitter near you" (matches hero.ts ctaLabel). Exit rule for all: search started, unsubscribe, or reply with a question.

**E1, day 0. Purpose:** welcome, say why they get it, start the search.
- Subject: Find a sitter for your pet at home
- Preview: See who is nearby, free to search.
- Body: You signed up for PetPal updates. PetPal connects owners with in-home pet sitters, so your pet stays in familiar surroundings. Searching is free and needs no card.
- CTA link: [Find a sitter near you](https://petpal.example/search?utm_source=petpal&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch-drip&utm_content=email1-welcome)

**E2, day 3. Purpose:** answer the stranger-in-my-home worry.
- Subject: How we check the sitters you can book
- Preview: ID checks and reviews, explained.
- Body: Every sitter is ID-checked, and you can read reviews from other pet owners before you choose. Compare sitters at your own pace.
- CTA link: [Find a sitter near you](https://petpal.example/search?utm_source=petpal&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch-drip&utm_content=email2-trust)

**E3, day 7. Purpose:** give a practical next step (interview questions).
- Subject: Questions worth asking any pet sitter
- Preview: A short list before your first booking.
- Body: Ask about emergencies, medication and daily updates before you book. Our guide lists the questions at petpal.example/guides (to be published; plain text, not a second CTA).
- CTA link: [Find a sitter near you](https://petpal.example/search?utm_source=petpal&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch-drip&utm_content=email3-questions)
