Keywords are unvalidated hypotheses (no research tools used); no statistics are cited. Machine-drafted: needs human editorial review. Claims such as "verified" await Defne's review. Ownership: hero and conversion copy is Kaan's, social is Jale's, structured data is Selin's.

## Pillar (TOFU/MOFU, ICP: urban owners 25-45 who distrust kennels)
**How to Choose a Trusted Pet Sitter: The Owner's Guide to Leaving Your Pet at Home** (`/guides/choose-a-pet-sitter`, 2,000+ words)

## Clusters (each links to the pillar with a descriptive anchor)
1. Pet Sitter vs. Kennel: Which Is Right for Your Pet? (comparison, MOFU)
2. 12 Questions to Ask a Pet Sitter Before the First Booking (checklist, MOFU)
3. How to Prepare Your Home and Pet for a Sitter's First Visit (how-to, MOFU)
4. What Does Pet Sitting Cost? A Line-by-Line Price Guide (commercial, BOFU)
5. Leaving a Pet With a Stranger: How ID Checks and Reviews Work (trust, BOFU)

## Brief 1
- **Working title:** Pet Sitter vs. Kennel: Which Is Right for Your Pet?
- **Target keyword:** "pet sitter vs kennel" (variants: "kennel alternatives", "in-home pet sitting")
- **Search intent:** commercial investigation, owner comparing options before a trip.
- **Audience:** urban owner of one or two pets who has used or dreads a kennel.
- **Angle:** fair side-by-side by pet type and stress level; concedes where a kennel fits.
- **Outline:** H2 decision factors; H2 comparison table; H2 cases where a kennel wins; H2 how to vet a sitter; H2 FAQ.
- **CTA:** one only: "Find a sitter near you" (sitter search start).
- **Internal links:** pillar; cluster 2; cluster 4 (descriptive anchors).
- **Sources needed:** veterinary or animal-welfare body guidance on boarding stress; PetPal's own vetting policy, confirmed by the team. Each cited with year and URL.
- **Success metric:** organic click-through to sitter search; threshold set after a baseline exists.

## Brief 2
- **Working title:** 12 Questions to Ask a Pet Sitter Before the First Booking
- **Target keyword:** "questions to ask a pet sitter" (variants: "pet sitter interview", "pet sitter checklist")
- **Search intent:** informational with booking intent; owner about to contact a sitter.
- **Audience:** same ICP; first-time sitter bookers anxious about strangers at home.
- **Angle:** a printable question set, each question paired with a good and a red-flag answer.
- **Outline:** H2 why interview; H2 experience and emergencies; H2 routines and meds; H2 communication and updates; H2 red flags; H2 FAQ.
- **CTA:** one only: "Message a sitter on PetPal".
- **Internal links:** pillar; cluster 5; cluster 3.
- **Sources needed:** pet first-aid and emergency guidance from a recognised vet or rescue body; PetPal's verification steps (Defne-reviewed). Year and URL for each.
- **Success metric:** search-started rate from readers; threshold set after instrumentation (see strategy.md).
