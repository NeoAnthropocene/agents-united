# Completion reporting with missing shutdown notices

Observed **2026-10-13**, Claude Code **2.1.296**, in the maintainer's Round 2 control/intent/restricted tests for PR #202. The executor read the supplied host records and settings-hook events; no new paid session ran. [Compact evidence](2026-10-13-completion-evidence.json) contains the lead IDs, accepted request times, first-report Stops, later human-stage times and notice counts. It contains no unrelated session names or transport addresses.

All three runs added the same completion paragraph to baseline agency roles. Intent guidance was absent in control and present in the other two; discovery was granted in control/intent and withheld in restricted. Each used the same two specialist instances for work and revision, the native structured shutdown protocol, and test containment. The settings Stop observer logged completion/waiting text without forcing an answer or refusing a Stop. All role and hook hashes matched preparation.

Each first report arrived before Stage 2, with no human shutdown reminder. Each included the verified deliverables and a shutdown evidence table marking absent approval and host termination as not observed. Peer records contained two approval calls per run; they did not all reach the lead record. Those outgoing calls, accepted request results and any user-observed UI termination are distinct evidence. The experiment does not independently establish that every teammate process exited or why notices were missing.

The restricted first report text was recorded at 15:53:12.406 UTC, its completion Stop at 15:53:12.483, and Ava's approval plus host termination at 15:53:12.504. The subsequent lead update at 15:53:15.047 said it was still waiting on Kaan. Kaan issued its approval at 15:53:15.023, with no matching host notice in the reviewed lead record. Thus **initial reporting succeeded in all three; remaining non-waiting behavior after a late event was violated in restricted**. It is not correct to label the entire flow flawless.

ADR 0055 carries the tested paragraph into the source role and adds a specific late-event rule: delivery remains complete, update only evidence, and do not wait, poll, wake a finished peer or ask the user for a missing notice. This extra clarification is **not yet verified live**. Failed or declined requests, full nine-specialist delivery and other hosts are also unverified live. Source/installed-role contract tests do not replace those observations.

This is a delivery-method correction, independent of the ListAgents decision. The 10-second experimental mode-line setting did not prevent every hold; there was no paired 8-second comparison, and the production default is unchanged. The three leader cost-state records were 0.9087 / 0.8690 / 0.9737 USD of notional model cost; the executor incurred no additional Claude-session cost.
