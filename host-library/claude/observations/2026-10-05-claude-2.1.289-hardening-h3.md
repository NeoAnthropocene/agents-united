## What was established

- **With no contract fixed in the briefs, the two teammates settled the interface by messaging each other, within the budget (the question of H3).** The records, in order (UTC): `deniz` messaged `kaan` with a proposal (13:38:51.664, "reply only if you want changes"), and `kaan` messaged `deniz` with his own proposal 0.34 s later (13:38:52.000): **the two proposals crossed**. `kaan` wrote `cta.copy.ts` on his own proposal (13:38:55.291) and `deniz` wrote `CtaButton.tsx` on his (13:38:58.451). `deniz` reported (13:39:04) that kaan had not replied. `kaan` (his report: "Deniz's proposal arrived after I had written the file") adopted it, rewrote `cta.copy.ts` (13:39:05.394) and told `deniz` "your proposal wins, please ignore mine" (13:39:06.733). Three messages for one pair (the budget is four), and **messages in both directions before either file was written**.
- **The files agree.** `cta.copy.ts` exports `CtaCopy` and `signupCta`; `CtaButton.tsx` imports exactly those, holds the event name `signup_cta_click` and the test id `cta-signup`, and takes `copy?`, `href?`, `location?`, `disabled?`, `onClick?`. `kaan`'s rewrite dropped the `analyticsEvent`, `testId`, `href` and `microcopy` fields of his first version.
- **A message to a working teammate arrives after its turn ends**, as on 2026-10-04: `deniz`'s proposal reached `kaan` only after `kaan` had written his file, so agreement cost one rewrite. The teammates did not wait for a reply, as the brief said.
- **Reports.** `kaan`'s report (an update that replaces the first) has `Peer messages received` and `Open items`. `deniz`'s report has `Open items` and a heading `Peer messages` instead of `Peer messages received`, so the helper's check for the two sections says no: a deviation in a heading, not a missing report.
- **Shutdown** was structured (two `shutdown_request`) and both teammates answered `approve: true` (13:39:11, 13:39:13); the helper counted one of them as received by the lead.
- **Gate: pass** with one deviation, the heading of `deniz`'s report.
- **H1c, a false positive and a real violation.** The helper flagged `deniz` and `kaan` as shell-less writers that did not re-read. `kaan` is shell-less and wrote `cta.copy.ts` twice without a `Read` (a real violation, #146). `deniz` holds a shell (`agency-frontend-architect` lists `Bash` and `PowerShell`); the helper decided "shell-less" from the absence of a shell *call* in the session, so `deniz` was flagged although the re-read rule does not apply to him: fixed in #147, which reads the role's tools.
