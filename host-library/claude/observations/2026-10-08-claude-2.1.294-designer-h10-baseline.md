## Unplanned observations

- **A false "no tokens" claim (H10c).** She wrote "Brand tokens and logo: none found in the project" and "the project has no brand tokens", while `docs/pilot/design-tokens.json` and `hero.ts` were there. Her first listing was cut at 100 of 691 files (the install's own `.claude/` files fill it; the notice says so) and her second pattern, `design-tokens.json`, can only match at the root. She then used a "provisional palette" of ink `#2B2118`, cream `#FFF6EA` and CTA `#B5472A`, near to the tokens (`#2B1D14`, `#FFF8F0`, `#B5451B`) and not the tokens, and said it was a guess. In H10b injected the same search with `**/design-tokens.json` found the file at once. The prompt of H10c does not name the path; the two H10a and H10b prompts do.
- **The host re-encodes the image and does not say so.** `Read` returned a JPEG of the 506 KiB PNG, as the host documentation says it does above 500 KB; the model has no way to see that, so "what I could not judge" cannot be answered from the image alone.
- **Her creative skills were barely exercised.** Across the four runs the only skill loaded was `ad-creative-design` (three runs). `design-system-tokens` and `marketing-creative-design` were never loaded, because the prompts did not ask for tokens or for brand assets; finding F5 still holds for both, and the H8 runs of S8 need prompts that call for them.
- **Re-reading.** H10a re-read three of nine files in full and grepped the headline lines, and said "the full text of the other six files was not re-read". H10c re-read both files in a later response than the writes. The rule says to re-read what she wrote; one of the two runs did it in part, and said so.
- **Disclosed deviations.** H10a drew the link ad's headline at 80 px against the token's 96 px ("96 px would not fit the 628 px height"), copied the token hex values by hand ("the SVGs do not read design-tokens.json"), and asked Defne whether an in-image "Book now" reads as a fake control. She wrote no copy: the headline and the hooks came from `hero.ts`.
- **A choice the prompt left open.** "The feed ad" of H10c gave no size; she drew 1:1 (1080 x 1080), the floor's first example, where H10b's placement is 4:5.
- **Where she wrote.** H10c wrote its two files in the project root, not under `docs/`; the prompt named no path.
- **Cost against estimate.** 1.0212 USD against about 2 USD. The suite used 62 percent of its cap and the other three runs 17 to 25 percent.
