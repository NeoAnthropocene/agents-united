## What Sittings O and P say

1. **The new path rule held in the one run that tested it.** With the full path typed, she looked at the file, made one call with exactly that path, said it went to Gemini and copied nothing; her own checker accepts that call with `--allow` and refuses it without. With a path only written in a brief (H10d4) she made the picture from the written brief, quoted the path and asked the user to type it if they meant it. Against a URL (H10d8) she made no call and said that she cannot fetch it.
2. **Four of the five misses of Sitting O were lines that her loaded text lacked, and one was a line that it had.** The record's keys, "I have seen nothing" and "where it comes from" were in no text she loads (the template, the paths reference and the pass line held them). The placeholder after a missing key was in `SKILL.md` ("offer the placeholder") and she prepared the call and waited: the line stood in a list of five, and her role text did not carry it. This is the first miss in four sittings that the text did ask for, and it is one run.
3. **The record is the weak point, a third time.** In Sitting L she wrote a free-form `.md`, in Sitting N a record with a null cost, and here a JSON record in her own keys: each time the run that did not open the template of `image-creation` wrote a record that the checker rejects, and each run that opened it wrote one that passes. The keys of the template now stand in step 5 of `SKILL.md`, which she always loads before a call, and her role text says which template to read.
4. **The lead's offer works when it can read the prices.** In H10k1 it asked once, with the cost worked out for the plan in every option, figures that agree with the checker, no teammate before the answer, her own route briefed as vector art and placeholders, and no key asked for.
5. **It does not when its reads are held.** In H10k2 the hook held five calls, the lead wrote a text turn that already asked the choice in prose and put the prices off, and the maintainer, shown no selection list and no figure, typed "option 1". The fault was in how the question was asked, not in the answer typed to it. The lead's paragraph now says that if its reads of the prices are held or fail it says so and waits, and never asks without the figures or in prose.
6. **The Gemini branch of the lead has not been seen.** The go-ahead with the user's ceiling, the command printed with a placeholder and run by nobody, and the restart with the check by `ToolSearch` belong to a session that chooses a provider, and neither did. The scripted answer ("choose the option that names Gemini") assumed a selection list and said nothing about a question asked in prose.
7. **The sitting cost more than planned and stopped late.** About 5.58 USD by the estimate (1.87 and 3.71), because the maintainer, in both sessions, accepted the delegation map instead of stopping at the lead's first report, and the team ran. The script now says to stop there.

## Unplanned observations

- In H10d4 she wrote "Ceiling: you gave none, so I treated the $0.80 session budget as the ceiling": the host shows a budget reminder only in a headless run with `--max-budget-usd`, so a real user never meets it. She said so and invited a correction.
- In H10k2 the lead ran `npx --yes -p typescript tsc --noEmit ...` twice to type-check the teammates' files, which fetches a package. The lead's rules about installing concern integrations (`mcp-setup`), not tools it fetches for its own checks. Nothing here decides whether they should.
- The mode-line hook held four calls in H10k1 and five in H10k2 (an expected hold, ADR 0044). Both lead sessions recovered; in H10k2 it cost the question its form.
- The first version of the reader of the interactive sessions doubled the tokens (it added a message once for every record that carried it), knew an "answer" only as the result of `AskUserQuestion`, counted teammates' notices as things the user typed, and flagged `npx tsc` and "I never ask for it" as failures. It is corrected, and the readings kept here are the corrected ones (`evidence-h10k1.txt`, `evidence-h10k2.txt`).
- In H10d3 the variation of a plain 64 x 64 tile is a still life; she said she could see no subject in the file, and told the user that it may not match.
