## The instruction "fixed in every brief" (H1)

**Violated, in five of nine delivery briefs, as in R3.** The brief fixes the colour token `color.cta.primary` and the test id `hero-primary-cta` "in every brief". The lead's briefs carry both for Ava (and her consultation), Kaan, Deniz and Emre; Jamileh's lacks the test id; Yavuz's, Selin's and Jale's lack the colour token; Defne's lacks both (`checks.txt`: 5 of 10 briefs with both values, 4 of the 9 delivery briefs). The outputs still use the right values, because the teammates read `strategy.md` and the task list, which carry them. The lead's final report says "Every teammate was told the CTA colour token is `color.cta.primary` and the CTA test id is `hero-primary-cta`", which the briefs do not show. Nothing in the lead's text was changed for this, and R3 had left it open.

## What R4 says

1. **The opening held on a full run.** Third interactive session in a row (Sitting V twice, now R4) in which the lead wrote its line in the second message and no gated call was held. With the headless trials this is the effect of the lead's text (ADR 0053). It is **not** evidence about the wait: the line was in the file at the first look in all three, so a slow save is still untested and the cap of 8 s has not met one.
2. **The full roster ran to the end on this build**: all nine roles, the consultation, the acceptance, four tiers in order and nine files, in 4 min 49 s. The seven artifact and runtime expectations of R3 are seen again.
3. **The end of the run races with the last shutdown confirmations, again** (R3: Defne; R4: Emre and Defne). The lead writes its final report as soon as the last requests are sent and says so honestly, and the session was closed in the seconds before the confirmations could be delivered. The scripted stop said to wait for the report to say that every teammate had shut down; it said two were pending. The script now says to wait for the lead's next short message. A lead that held its final report until the last `teammate_terminated` would close the gap; that is not built.
4. **`ListAgents` returned the maintainer's other sessions to the lead.** The lead called `ListAgents` once (before it spawned the second Ava) and the result listed four of the maintainer's other sessions by title, local and cloud. The lead's `tools` hold `ListAgents` and `SendMessage`, and the host lets `SendMessage` address other sessions. The lead used the result for nothing visible. Whether a Tier-2 lead should be able to enumerate and message sessions that are not its team is a question for the maintainer; it is not changed here, and the line is removed from the kept timeline.
5. **Two Avas ran in turn under one name** (the consultant, shut down at 09:10:16Z, then the Ava who wrote, spawned at 09:10:59Z); the lead said which shutdown was which ("That shutdown was the first Ava, the consultant"). No confusion followed.
