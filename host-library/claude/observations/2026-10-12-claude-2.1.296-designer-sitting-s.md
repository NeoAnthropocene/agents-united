## What Sitting S says

1. **Both sessions failed, for the same reason.** In both the lead's second message held the `ToolSearch` that loads the task tools, as ADR 0051 says, and no text: the line and the introduction stood in its reasoning ("Running in Limited Operational mode with ..."; "As Chris, lead of the digital agency team, ..."). **The order of ADR 0051 was not followed in either session.** An instruction to write text before a call does not make the model write it: a model that means to go on to a call keeps its words in its reasoning, as in Sittings Q and R. The order is a preferred path and not a guarantee.
2. **The first call other than `ToolSearch` was held, and the message of the hold kept it held.** The message said that a lead that had written the line as a text block should repeat the call alone, and the lead's section said never to write the line a second time. Both leads believed that they had written the line, and obeyed: H10k2 repeated its read nine times and stopped, G1 repeated its call ten times and stopped.
3. **The installed hook, replayed on each final transcript, answers "held".** The saved transcripts hold 4 text blocks of the lead in H10k2 and 3 in G1, and none opens with the line (`replay-hook-h10k2.txt`, `replay-hook-g1.txt`; exit 2). **The hook was right and the lead was wrong about what it had written.** Its account of itself was false in both sessions ("I wrote the line twice", "I wrote the mode line as a text block at the start of my second message"), and in H10k2 it blamed the hook and suggested disabling it, when the hook was doing what it was written to do.
4. **The wrong turn was decision 3 of ADR 0051**, which the executor took together with decision 1 and which the maintainer had not asked for: to stop the line being written twice, it removed "even if you believe you wrote it already" from the message of the hold and made the message conditional on the lead's own report of what it had written. That sentence had been the net for a lead whose failure is a false report, which live test 6 (session `1aa049c5`, Claude Code 2.1.292, a lead that answered "my last message already began with the mode line" after a message with no text), Sitting Q (three messages) and Sitting R (its first message) had already shown. The risk was known when decision 3 was written and it was shipped. Decision 1 is the other half: it makes the right behaviour likely, and cannot make it certain.
5. **Every call was held, `AskUserQuestion` included.** The lead could not ask its question with the tool, and asked it in prose; that is a consequence of the loop and not of the paragraph of the lead (in Sitting R, held twice, it made two `AskUserQuestion` calls). Nor could it load `image-generation` to read the prices, so no figure was given.
6. **The amended hook, replayed on the two final transcripts, counts 4 and 3 text blocks** (`replay-amended-hook.txt`, three holds in a row on each): the first message asks for the line as a text block even if the lead believes it wrote it, the second lets a lead whose last message opened a text block with the line repeat the call alone, and the third says how many text blocks the hook sees and that none opens with the line. A lead that follows the order is held 0 times; one that follows the first message is held twice in the usual case, when the host saves the transcript within a model round trip; the line is released only by the line in the saved transcript (ADR 0044), so a lead that never writes it stays held. This is built after the sitting and has not been run live.
7. **The sitting cost 0.9458 USD in 2 prompts** and reached neither the offer nor the consultation.
