# ADR 0037: The Created Lane Is Retired, and Its Assertions Live on the Native Files

- **Status**: Accepted — 2026-10-04 (maintainer, in chat, after a grilling: "class grants supersede H3 for native roles", "declare the Tier-1 comms invariants as deltas", "everything only the created lane used goes"). Follow-up (3) of the plan 032 close-out; it carries out the part of decision B1 (2026-10-03) that said `src/core/creation/claude.ts` is retired only after its assertions are ported to the native files. Builds on ADR 0021 (the Semantic Core and the creation engine, strangler step 1), ADR 0025 (native host packages) and ADR 0036.
- **Context**: Prior art searched first: the importers of `createRole` (only `tests/helpers/created-golden.ts`, used by four test files), `registry/realizations/claude/*.json` and `registry/profiles/claude@2.1.271.json` (read by the created lane and one host-profile test), the dialect's `invariantBindings` (read by the created lane and one comms test), the CI step that ran the created suites, ADR 0021 and `docs/creation-engine.md`, and plan 032 decision B1. The lane generated five Claude role files from a Semantic Core, a binding table and a capability profile, and pinned them as goldens. The native lane since replaced it: the nine committed native files are authored, their floor block is generated from the core, and install copies them. The created lane no longer shipped anything. What its tests asserted, and where each part stands now: **floor identity** (native files and legacy projections, both still true), **determinism and goldens** (of a generator that no longer exists), **declared deltas** (the realization files' above-floor scope), **the parity gate** (every core invariant carries a bound mechanic; the legacy lane delegates through `Agent` and hands back through `SubagentHandback`; tool citations in a bound mechanic exist in the legacy lane), **least privilege** (Plan 022 H2 and H3 on the created output) and **the comms invariants** (the dialect binds each; the created output carries each). Four facts surfaced while porting, none visible before: native `frontend-architect` holds `TaskCreate`, `TaskUpdate` and `TodoWrite` through its capability classes, which Plan 022 H3 forbade and the legacy and created lanes enforce; the three Tier-1 native specialists bind none of the peer-messaging invariants their cores state; the three agency teammates' bodies did not bind the planning-consultation invariant (it was in their cores only); and `backend-architect`'s own steps list implementation before tests, against its core's test-first invariant.
- **Decision**:
  1. **Port first, then delete.** The assertions that are still true were ported and passing before anything was removed (commit order: the new coverage suite red then green, the ports, then the retirement).
  2. **The parity gate becomes invariant coverage.** Every invariant a native role's core states is either evidenced in the committed file (`tests/helpers/native-invariant-evidence.ts`: one rule per invariant, role-specific where the wording differs, each quoting a sentence of the floor or the body) or declared in `registry/hosts/claude/deltas.json` with a disposition and a rationale. Silence fails (`validateDeclaredDeltas`); a delta is stale, and fails, when the body does evidence the invariant; a rule that matches empty or unrelated text fails. 95 role-invariant pairs, 44 distinct invariants, nine roles. The legacy lane's `Agent` and `SubagentHandback` checks and the ledger entries for `schedule` and `manage_task` stay as stand-alone assertions.
  3. **Least privilege is the class ceiling.** A native role holds exactly the tools of its capability classes (asserted by the host-profile suite: nothing to add, nothing hand-granted). H2 stays: no specialist holds `Agent` or a scheduling tool. H3 stays for the read-only roles (`permissionMode: plan`, no mutating tool). **The Plan 022 H3 line for `frontend-architect` ("no `TaskCreate`") is superseded for native roles by the class grant** (maintainer decision): it keeps the Task tools its `task-tracking` class grants and holds no scheduling tool; the legacy lane keeps its own H3 assertions.
  3a. **Tier-1 comms are declared deltas.** The Tier-1 specialists stay hub-and-spoke (the maintainer's standing decision), so the invariants they do not bind (check delivered peer messages, the handoff report's peer sections, direct messaging only in team mode, the two-exchange budget) are declared with their rationale: stricter than the invariant, or bound by the lead's brief. Tier 2 and `frontend-architect` evidence all of them.
  4. **What was deleted**: `src/core/creation/claude.ts`, its test and the golden helper, the five created goldens, `registry/realizations/`, `registry/profiles/claude@2.1.271.json` (the version floor stays as `minVersion` in `registry/hosts/claude/profile.json`, and its `legacyProfile` pointer is gone), the dialect's `capabilityProfile` and `invariantBindings` with their types, and the created-lane section of three tests. The CI conformance step runs the Semantic Core, native conformance and invariant coverage suites instead. `docs/creation-engine.md` is now a retirement note.
  5. **What stays**: the legacy projection lane and its goldens (54 of 59 agents), `validateContractFloor` and `validateDeclaredDeltas`, the translation ledger, the dialect's vocabulary maps, the Semantic Core.
- **Consequences**:
  - Positive: one lane of native files with its conformance on the committed text; an invariant that a body stops stating fails a test; two real gaps found by the port are fixed or declared.
  - Negative: invariant evidence is a regular expression over prose, so rewording a body means updating the table (intended friction, with a specificity check against vacuous rules); a native file is authored, so there is no generator whose determinism could be pinned (install is byte-stable, tested); the old Claude tool-surface snapshot (2.1.271) and the dialect's binding wording survive only in git history, and that wording ("messages are read between turns") was disproven for teammates by the Agent Teams run.
  - Open: `backend-architect` implements before it tests (declared, for the maintainer to reorder); whether the Tier-1 specialists should bind the report sections in their own bodies (declared as bound by the lead's brief); the projectors and `FEATURE_LEDGER`, which retire per bundle when its roles are native.
