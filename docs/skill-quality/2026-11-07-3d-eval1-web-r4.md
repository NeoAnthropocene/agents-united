## Cap defect and correction

The original `create-bottle.mjs` assigned every profile side strip to jade;
only the upper disk belonged to the brass primitive. The viewer faithfully
reproduced that supplied GLB. This is a fixture defect, not a viewer material
substitution. The new geometric regression first failed with jade on cap
faces. The minimal correction assigns the flare and wall above the neck at
2.05 m to brass, preserves the jade neck/body and all **960 triangles**, and
identifies the generator as **1.0.1**.

The corrected technical proof is separate:
`scratch/3d-cap-fix-2026-11-07/output/proof/frame-000000.png`.
Actual Khronos inspection and Chrome rendering exited 0; the executor viewed
the new PNG and confirmed brass sides/top. The maintainer then reviewed that
corrected PNG and answered **“Yes, acceptable for this prototype”** on
2026-11-07. This accepts the cap sides/top for the prototype; it does not
establish Motion playback, final sound or commercial readiness. The existing
r4 eval2 source, sequence and
fingerprint are intentionally preserved for its fixed-input handoff exercise.
