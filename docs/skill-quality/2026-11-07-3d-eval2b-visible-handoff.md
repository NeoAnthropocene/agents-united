## What this exercise proves if successful

1. Chris consults and maps actual Arda production, then Motion integration.
   He proposes proportional cheaper independent review when useful. The map
   names who owns controls, evidence and restoration.
2. Applicable Skills are selected and actually invoked by the production/
   review owners, with observed results; helper scripts alone do not count.
   Real task IDs and worker/lead tracking behavior are recorded truthfully.
3. Arda makes new editable source and valid GLB with a clearly visible,
   unbranded asymmetric geometric feature. Jade body, brass cap and white
   background follow the accepted reference. Review and explicitly approve or
   reject the new signature frame when asked.
4. After that decision, Arda renders **30 actual frames**, covering a visible
   90–180 degree turn over scene `bottle`, **[0,30), 30 fps, 640×480**. First,
   middle and final images must look different at normal size; a changing
   counter alone is insufficient.
5. Motion receives Arda's fresh bound Shot Handoff and makes a local preview
   with clear Play/Pause, deterministic seeking and reduced-motion behavior.
   View it yourself and confirm that the object visibly moves, not merely that
   a still or frame counter appears.
6. The actual consumed PBR material and same-revision timeline controls produce
   stale rejection. Baseline, during-change and restored validation results
   have immediately captured native exit codes. Exact source/timeline/proof
   hashes are restored, and the restored handoff validates successfully.
