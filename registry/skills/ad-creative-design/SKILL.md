# Ad Creative Design Playbook

## Overview & Purpose
`ad-creative-design` provides standardized design frameworks and visual hierarchies for digital advertising campaigns across Meta, Google Display, LinkedIn, and X.

## Core Directives & Standards
1. **Visual Hierarchy** — Lead with a high-contrast visual hook (top 40% of viewport), followed by a 1-sentence value proposition, social proof badge, and an actionable CTA.
2. **Aspect Ratio Compliance** — Export designs in 1:1 (Feed/Square), 9:16 (Stories/Reels/Shorts), and 1.91:1 (Landscape/Feed).
3. **Contrast & Readability** — Maintain WCAG AA compliant text contrast against background gradients or imagery.
4. **Multi-Hook Variation** — Provide at least 3 distinct visual angle variations per campaign concept (Product Demo screenshot, Stat/Metric Callout, Minimalist Typography).

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Visual elements scale cleanly across mobile screen viewports.
- [ ] Text covers less than 20% of ad image area to maximize ad delivery score.
- [ ] CTA button stands out with clear accent color contrast.
