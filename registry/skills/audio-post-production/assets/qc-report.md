# Audio finishing QC template

For wordless audio, mark speech, script/transcript, captions, pronunciation and voice-consent fields not applicable with a reason; no invented narration or Kaan approval. Included speech requires Kaan-approved exact words and voice consent. Source rights and required applicable audio/provenance review remain mandatory.

## Numerical review

Record actual master/stem decoded sample frames, duration, sample rate, channels, frame-zero alignment, cue silence boundaries and retained gains. State rounding where frame boundaries are noninteger sample positions. Record remeasured master LUFS/true peak and target/tolerance/ceiling acceptance; report failures without substituting desired values. Identify pre-master versus nonlinear master stem behavior.

## Listening and content review

Reviewer/date, comfortable playback conditions/devices, mono check, exact words/pronunciation, intelligibility, noise/clicks, music/effect balance, sync and tails. Include timecoded finding, severity, remedy, owner and re-review status. If unperformed, write **listening unverified**. Creative and rights approvals each have their own owner/status.

## Standalone delivery or film hand-off and mux review

Master/stems/manifest, cue sheet, applicable exact transcript/captions, source/process/permission evidence and editable automation policy. Standalone WAV/sting: required applicable audio/provenance review and direct delivery to Chris; no Motion, storyboard, renderer, video or mux prerequisite. Film: Motion confirms renderer and exact revision/fingerprint; record final mux path/hash, presentation start/duration, decoded sample count/padding, remeasured audio and named sync/listening findings. Required missing checks stay **unverified** and block acceptance; irrelevant checks need a not-applicable reason. Route copy/timing/preset changes through Chris and the applicable owner; reissue dependent handoffs and any renderer exports.
