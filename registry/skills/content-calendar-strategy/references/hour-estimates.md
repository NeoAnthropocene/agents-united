# Hour estimates and the capacity rule

Rough ranges for what an item costs a small team. They are estimates: replace them with the team's own history as soon as there is one, and label them as estimates until then.

| Item | Hours (rough range) | Also count |
|---|---|---|
| Pillar page | 12 to 20 | engineer or subject-expert review, editing, design |
| Cluster article | 4 to 8 | editing, a link up to the pillar |
| Case study | 8 to 12 | the customer's approval time, which is not your hours and is slow |
| Refresh of a page that already ranks | about 3 (the worked example) | the 90-day review: dates, facts, links, intent against the current top results |
| Derived items for distribution (social, email) | counted separately for Jale | dated after the pillar is published |

## The capacity rule

- Capacity = hours per week by role x weeks in the plan (13 weeks for 90 days).
- Schedule at most 80 percent of it. The rest is slack for illness, review that runs long and a release that moves.
- If the total is well under 80 percent, add a pillar. If it is over, say which items slip first.
