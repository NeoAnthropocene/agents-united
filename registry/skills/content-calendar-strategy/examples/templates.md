# Templates

Copy a block and fill every line. A line you cannot fill is a gap: name it and who can close it.

## The capacity check

```text
CAPACITY   writer <h/wk>  editor <h/wk>  designer <h/wk>  expert review <h/wk>  legal review <h/wk>   x <weeks> = <total h>
PLAN TO    <80 percent of total> h  (the rest is slack)
SCHEDULED  <pillars> + <clusters> + <case studies and guides> + <refreshes> + <distribution, design and review> = <h>
ASSUMPTION <if capacity was not given: state it here, and plan to a smaller number>
```

## The calendar (one row per item; an item with no owner is not scheduled)

```text
| Wk | Item | Keyword (source, date read) | Search intent | Format | Owner | Reviewer | Needs (dependencies) | Gate | Publish | Channels |
```

Gates: brief approved, draft, review, published. Pillar rows come before their cluster rows.

## The refresh list (pages that already earn traffic, reviewed at 90 days)

```text
| Page | Traffic (source, date) | What to check: dates, facts, links, intent against the current top results | Owner | Wk |
```

## What slips first

```text
IF CAPACITY DROPS, THESE SLIP FIRST, IN ORDER: <item>, <item>, <item>
```
