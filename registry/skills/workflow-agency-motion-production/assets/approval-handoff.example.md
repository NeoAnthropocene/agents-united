# Example approval handoff (fictional)

- gate: relevant timeline timing
- status: pending; user approval unverified
- approver: user, unless expressly delegated
- source/reference: none supplied
- revision: timeline r1 and its computed fingerprint
- open items: obtain scoped timing evidence; listening and perceived sync unverified

A later explicit scoped waiver is recorded as a waiver, not human review. A lead decision is identified separately; route and tone choices do not establish audio-map acceptance or listening. Numerical results are reported separately from actual review.
