# Worked example: local product-page launch film

The campaign specialist proposes `launch-film`: 15 seconds, 16:9 MP4, product page, trial users, existing `assets/ui.png`, `brief/copy.md` and `brief/brand.json`. It is optional and pending in the supplied request example. Chris consolidates it with the wider map; the human may include, defer or decline. This fictional example does not supply actual approval, source rights or assets.

If included, Creative confirms the approved screenshot/tokens and Copy fixes the exact script. Chris records Native, local audio and no generated footage with a zero API budget, using the complete preference example. Run `project.mjs init --project <absolute-project> --preferences <absolute-approved-json>` once, then `status --project <absolute-project>`. Both helpers validate the full preferences; an existing record is inspected rather than overwritten. For another destination or API route, approve the changed complete record before use. No provider is required for this local path.

Motion drafts a 900-frame/60fps storyboard: problem [0,180), useful result [180,660), CTA [660,900). Sound checks scratch narration against the exact copy before timing approval. These ranges belong in a complete canonical timeline with all required fields; they are not a partial replacement for the strict schema. Once Chris records timing approval, Motion animates and Sound prepares approved supplied/recorded sources, edits and final mixing in parallel.

Sound hands over the exact timeline revision/fingerprint, stereo48k WAV master, aligned full-duration stems, cues, captions/transcript, provenance and actual measurement/listening evidence. Motion validates it, renders, and hands the actual export to Creative/Copy, QA and Compliance. Chris gets the review record, source/export paths and unresolved decisions. If a VO word changes, reissue timing and the dependent Sound handoff before integration. Treating a saved Native preference or a scratch voice as final acceptance is an anti-pattern. Rendering, playback, listening, rights and actual Host behavior are **unverified** in this example.

## Sound-only alternative

For an included three-second WAV sonic logo, Chris records renderer null, format wav and the applicable transcript/accessibility needs in the Media Request. Sound reuses approved sources and direction, obtains cue/timing approval and chooses local/API/hybrid work under its actual authorizations. Keep the complete project schema, but its renderer/video defaults are unused. Sound owns the cue/timing record, final WAV/stems/manifest/provenance and measured/listening evidence, then hands applicable audiovisual-review results directly to Chris. No Motion assignment, storyboard, renderer install, MP4 or fabricated speech is required. This alternative is guidance, not an executed audio proof.
