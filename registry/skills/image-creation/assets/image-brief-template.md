# Image brief

Fill one per image. A brief for rung 4 goes to whoever supplies the picture; for rung 3 it is the record of what you asked the model for.

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Asset id | `<client>-<where>-<what>`, lower case, the same for every attempt (`petpal-hero-sofa`) |
| Placement | Platform and slot, final pixels, and the `aspectRatio` that fits (4:5 for a 1080 x 1350 feed) |
| Subject | One sentence: who or what, doing what |
| Story and mood | What the picture should make the viewer feel, in a few words |
| Setting | Where, and what surrounds the subject |
| Light and camera | Light direction, quality and time of day; lens, height, distance, focus |
| Palette in words | The tokens as colour words (cream, warm sand, one muted terracotta) |
| Copy zone | Where the headline sits, kept plain: "upper third, an even cream wall" |
| Must not contain | Text or numbers, logos, other brands, real or named people, the client's product |
| Rung and why | 1 supplied, 2 licensed, 3 generated, 4 placeholder, and the reason you stopped there |
| Source and licence | Rungs 1 and 2: where the picture comes from, the licence, who holds the rights |
| Generation settings | Rung 3: `imageSize`, `quality` (normally unset), the estimate, attempts used |
| Go-ahead | Who said go, their words, how many images, the ceiling |
| Placeholder frame | Rung 4: the label drawn in the design, `PHOTO <asset id> <ratio>` |
| Used in | The design files that place it |

A placeholder with this brief filled in is a complete deliverable: whoever supplies the picture can work from it, and the design already shows where it goes and what stays clear.
