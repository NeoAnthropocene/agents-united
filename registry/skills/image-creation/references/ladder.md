# The sourcing ladder

A photograph reaches a design by one of four rungs. Take them in order and stop at the first that works.

1. **Supplied or owned**: the client's own product and brand photography, with its rights recorded. Best: a real product and a known licence.
2. **Licensed stock the user chooses**, with the licence recorded. Never "found by image search, sort the licence out later".
3. **Generated**, only when `ToolSearch` lists `mcp__image-gen__generate_image` **and** the user or the lead has said go. It spends money and sends the words out. At most two regenerations per asset. Any text is an SVG or HTML overlay. A provenance file beside the image. The label the placement requires.
4. **Placeholder**: a labelled `PHOTO` frame in the design (its id and ratio) and an image brief ([../assets/image-brief-template.md](../assets/image-brief-template.md)) for whoever supplies the picture.

Say in the report which rung each image is on and why you stopped there: "rung 4, no photography supplied and the tool is not listed".

## What may be generated

| Subject | Generate it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A scene, a setting, a mood (a sofa in morning light, a park path) | Yes | A scene that illustrates a feeling misleads nobody |
| A texture or a background to carry copy | Yes | It makes no claim |
| An object in a setting that is not the client's product | Yes, kept generic | No readable brand, label or sign |
| The client's product | No | The real product is photographed. A generated one promises a look the product lacks; use a placeholder |
| A person in the scene | Only unnamed and not identifiable; a back, hands or a crop at the shoulders is safest | An invented face beside a name or a quote reads as a real person |
| A customer, reviewer, endorser, expert or staff member | Never | It invents someone who never said or did it. Use a real, consenting person's photo, or no picture |
| A real person, a public figure or a lookalike | Never | Likeness and consent |
| A logo, a mark, a third party's brand or trademark | No | Draw the client's own mark in SVG (`brand-identity`); never imitate another's |
| A chart, a diagram, a screenshot or an interface | No | They must be exact: write them in SVG or HTML |
| A result, a before and after, or a medical, financial or safety outcome | No | It is a claim, and the floor forbids deceptive creatives |

Never a generated or stock face as a customer, reviewer or endorser. Never an SVG drawn to look like a photograph: it fails on both counts, as a drawing and as a deception.

## Choosing between rungs 3 and 4

- The brief wants a feeling, a place or a texture, the tool is listed and the user said go: rung 3.
- The brief wants the product, a person who exists, a place that exists (the shop front) or a result: rung 1, 2 or 4. A placeholder with a good image brief is a complete deliverable.
- The user says go but the tool is not listed: rung 4, and say that the server is not connected. Do not try other routes to a picture.
