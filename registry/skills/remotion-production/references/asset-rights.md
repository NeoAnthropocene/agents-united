# Asset rights: proportionate checks and handoff

Reuse the user's already recorded intended use and rights facts; ask only for missing or changed facts. Record the experimental scope and each actually used asset in the [used-asset ledger](../assets/used-assets.example.md). Reuse evidence only while the exact source, version, hash, adaptations and intended use still match; recheck changed facts. A choice of personal or noncommercial use is not permission.

- **Personal-private:** make one quick source/terms lookup per unresolved asset using available evidence, not an exhaustive commercial audit. Record unknowns, scope, owner and next action. Unresolved terms alone do not stall the whole private draft; this is not a finding of permission.
- **Noncommercial public:** surface unresolved items before publication and offer replacement or owner review. Do not treat silence as clearance.
- **Commercial:** clear the actual intended use before delivery. An unresolved item blocks release of the affected asset; replace it or obtain owner review and continue unrelated work. Record the evidence and decision for the actual scope.

Known restrictions still apply in every scope; block affected use where required and continue unaffected work. Unknown terms are neither permission nor evidence of public-domain or free use. `user-reported-permission` is attributed to the reporting user and is not independently verified. `terms-unverified` means terms are not verified; `no-license-stated` means no licence was stated in the checked source, not that permission exists.

The [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/) describes noncommercial purpose as one factor in fair use, not automatic permission. This is a U.S. illustration, not legal advice or a determination for every jurisdiction or asset.

Keep runtime/tool/vendor eligibility and licence evidence separate from asset rights, and voice consent separate from both. Reuse a still-applicable eligibility record across workers, but recheck changes in its scope. No automatic system licence bypass is permitted; preserve existing evaluation, production and entitlement gates.

For unknown terms, include this warning once in the ledger and once in the handoff: **Check the commercial usage availability of the assets.** Identify affected assets, status, scope, owner and next action; do not repeatedly ask the same resolved question. Do not put credentials or private consent documents in the ledger; reference controlled evidence without secrets.
