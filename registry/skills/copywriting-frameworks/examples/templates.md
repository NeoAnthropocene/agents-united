# Templates

Copy a block and fill every line. A line you cannot fill is a gap: name it and who can close it.

## Intake (ask for it, never invent it)

```text
AUDIENCE     <who>, in the state <unaware | problem-aware | solution-aware | ready to buy>
OFFER        <what is on offer>
FACTS        3 to 5 true, specific facts (numbers, outcomes, constraints, who uses it), each with its source
THEIR WORDS  real customer language: tickets, reviews, interview quotes
VOICE        existing copy to learn the voice from (5 to 20 samples if they exist)
ACTION       the one thing the piece should make the reader do
```

## Voice profile

```text
SENTENCES    <short | long | mixed>             CLAIMS    <how direct>
NUMBERS      <how often, in what form>          NEVER SAYS <what the brand avoids>
SAMPLES      <count and where from>   or: UNCONFIRMED, written plainly
```

## Claim audit (one row per factual statement)

```text
| Statement | Source (file path, page, date) | Verdict (keep | opinion | remove) |
```

## Handover

```text
CLAIM        <the single claim, one checkable sentence>
FRAMEWORK    <name> because <reader state>
COPY         <the piece>
TO           <Jale | Jamileh (layout, hierarchy noted) | Deniz (typed props, if asked) | Defne (numbers, comparisons, health, finance, earnings claims)>
```
