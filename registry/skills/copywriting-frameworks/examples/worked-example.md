# Worked example (invented numbers, for checking your own work)

Brief: an invoicing tool for freelancers; the audience is problem-aware. Facts: invoices are created in 45 seconds on average (product telemetry, last 30 days); reminders are sent automatically; 3.1 percent of invoices are paid late among users with reminders against 11 percent without (internal analysis, a sample of 2,300; invented here); no card is needed for the free plan.

## Weak

"The all-in-one invoicing solution that streamlines your workflow and helps you get paid faster."

## The claim and the framework

Claim: "Freelancers who turn on reminders are paid late far less often." Framework: PAS.

## Strong

```text
Headline:   Chasing late invoices costs you the work you were paid for.
Problem:    Eleven in a hundred invoices arrive late when nobody reminds the client.
Agitate:    Each awkward email is an hour you did not bill.
Solve:      Turn on reminders once. Late invoices fell to three in a hundred for the people who did.
Proof:      Based on 2,300 invoices from users on the free plan, last quarter.
CTA:        Send your first invoice, free (no card).
```

## The claim audit and the hand-offs

"Eleven in a hundred" and "three in a hundred" tie to the internal analysis (the source file path goes in the evidence list); "free (no card)" ties to the pricing page.
