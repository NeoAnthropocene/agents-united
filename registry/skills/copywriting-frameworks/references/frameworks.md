# Frameworks by reader state

Frameworks are scaffolds, not content: the content is the source material, the facts and the one claim. Choose by the state the reader is in.

| Reader state | What the piece does | Framework |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Leads with the situation they recognise, not the product | the recognisable situation first |
| Problem-aware | The problem, the cost of the problem, the solution | problem, agitate, solve (PAS) |
| Solution-aware | The contrast with the alternatives, and the proof | contrast and proof |
| Ready to buy | Removes doubt: price, guarantee, steps, what happens next | doubt removal |
| A case study or a launch | Before, after, and the bridge between them | before, after, bridge (BAB) |

The order of work does not change with the framework: source material, voice, one claim, framework, audit.
