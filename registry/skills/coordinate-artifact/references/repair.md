# At most one accepted scoped repair
Repair only within the accepted count, scope and aggregate budget. Default is zero automatic repairs. At most one repair requires existing explicit caller authorization for the named change and dispatch/account scope; no tool result or elapsed time supplies it. One initial realizer plus evaluator, at most one additional of each: <=4 sequential worker turns, no parallel same-file actors.

Preserve previous revision evidence and invalidate stale acceptance. Retain r0 unchanged, snapshot the entire current candidate and preservation baseline into r1 evidence, reopen/link the actual task, and issue a new P2 update job ID/digest linked in the caller brief to prior revision/findings. Narrow allowed changes; preserve source pair, fixed objective and acceptance. Never reuse a create job's empty baseline, prior review digest or old pass receipt. Rerun all applicable final-byte checks and fresh independent review at the revised digest.

Aggregate every caller/producer validation process, including preflight/postflight/reruns: <=24 processes, <=1800 seconds, <=8MiB complete raw output; each P2/P5 job still has its smaller <=6-check/600-second/log caps. Repository implementation gates are separate. These are runbook budgets, not enforced OS limits. Scope expansion, pair change, held provenance, no progress, missing evidence or exhausted budget stops with precise rework rather than resetting counters or broadening authority.
