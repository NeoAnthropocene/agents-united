# At most one accepted scoped repair
Repair only within the accepted count, scope and per-job budgets. Default is zero automatic repairs. At most one repair requires existing explicit caller authorization for the named change and dispatch/account scope; no tool result or elapsed time supplies it. One initial realizer plus evaluator, at most one additional of each: <=4 sequential worker turns, no parallel same-file actors. Worker turns count worker dispatches; the coordinator's own turns while it waits for a hand-back, and the human's messages, do not consume them.

Preserve previous revision evidence and invalidate stale acceptance. Retain r0 unchanged, snapshot the entire current candidate and preservation baseline into r1 evidence, reopen/link the actual task, and issue a new P2 update job ID/digest linked in the caller brief to prior revision/findings. Narrow allowed changes; preserve source pair, fixed objective and acceptance. Never reuse a create job's empty baseline, prior review digest or old pass receipt. Rerun all applicable final-byte checks and fresh independent review at the revised digest.

A journey has no aggregate process, time or output budget, because nothing counts one. Its bounds are the turn and repair limits above and each P2/P5 job's own caps (<=6 checks, <=600 seconds and its log cap): the P2 handback checker compares the check count, seconds and log bytes with the receipts, the P5 review checker compares the check count and log bytes, and no checker compares the P5 seconds cap with a record. Repository implementation gates are separate. Scope expansion, pair change, held provenance, no progress, missing evidence or an exhausted per-job budget stops with precise rework rather than resetting counters or broadening authority.
