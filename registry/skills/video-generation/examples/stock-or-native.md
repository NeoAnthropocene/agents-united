# Worked example: unavailable service on a native project

An approved native HTML/SVG/canvas launch film needs a four-second atmosphere plate. Generated footage is an optional idea, but no account is connected and no upload or paid job is authorized. Preserve the approved 60fps timeline, exact words, brand/logo and local sound decisions. Report provider access **unverified**.

Chris offers the supplied library, commercially licensed stock research through `commercial-stock-sourcer`, or a procedural native background. For stock, record the actual licence, source/asset ID, permitted placement/territory, original local file/hash and crop/trim. For native motion, use original editable shapes/materials and the existing seek(t) contract. The absence of a selected provider never starts a different cloud service automatically.

After the human selects a source, Motion checks its actual duration/fps/codec and visible fit, records scene bounds and verifies the local composition. If the proposed shot changes timing, route that difference through Chris/Sound before freeze. A future generated-shot decision requires complete selected preferences, explicit reference permission, actual tools/auth and per-job acknowledgment first. No generation, playback or listening occurred in this example; the handoff is a reviewed decision and asset requirements, not a claimed film.
