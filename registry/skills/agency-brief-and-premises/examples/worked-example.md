# Worked example (an invented client, for checking your own work)

The client's message: "We need more signups for our invoicing tool before the end of the quarter."

## 1. The classification, said aloud

**Standard**, because it needs growth analysis, copy and tracking checks, three specialists. Not Programme: there is no launch and no fixed budget stated. The client can overrule it.

## 2. The restated brief

- **You said**: more signups, end of quarter, an invoicing tool.
- **I assume**: signups means free-plan accounts; the site has analytics.
- **Unknown**: the current signup number, what has been tried, who can change the site.

## 3. One question

"What would make you call this a success: a number of signups, or paying customers?" The answer: paying customers, 40 a month by the end of the quarter, from about 12 today.

## 4. The premises, put to the client

1. "Signups are the bottleneck": **disagree**, activation is 22 percent per their dashboard.
2. "Paid ads are the right channel": agree with doubt, there is no data (the cheapest check: Ava reads the channel numbers, read-only).
3. "Three people can change the site this month": agree.

## 5. The approaches

| Approach | What they get | Specialists | Effort | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A minimal | a funnel audit and three experiments on activation | Ava, Kaan, Emre | S | low: no new traffic |
| B ideal | A plus a referral loop and an SEO cluster | A plus Selin, Yavuz | L | higher: slower, needs content |
| C lateral | pause acquisition, fix the first-run experience only | Kaan, Deniz | M | the client may feel nothing is happening |

Recommendation: **A**, because the stated goal is paying customers and the data shows the leak is after signup. The client accepts A.

## 6. The brief and the next action

Objective: 40 paying customers a month by 2026-12-31, from 12 now. Success metric: paid conversions per month. Out of scope: changes to paid acquisition. Constraint: three people on site changes. The client's next action: export the funnel counts for the last 28 days by Friday.

Only now does the lead consult a specialist read-only and issue the delegation map.
