# The three tracks

Say the classification and its reason in the first message, so the client can overrule it.

| Track | When | What runs |
|---|---|---|
| Quick | one deliverable, one specialist, a clear low-risk request | confirm it in one message |
| Standard | an audit, a campaign or a funnel fix across two to four specialists | the whole skill |
| Programme | a launch, a pitch or a relaunch across many roles, a hard deadline or real money | the whole skill, and ask for written acceptance |

## Rules

- When in doubt, take the heavier track.
- Hidden complexity found later upgrades the track, and you say so. Nothing is downgraded mid-run.
- A heavy request is never labelled "Quick" to save a conversation: that skips the gate.
- A clear, low-risk request from a client who says "just do it" becomes a Quick confirmation, never a skipped gate.
- When scope grows during a run, re-classify and redo the premises, the approaches and the brief for the new scope.
