# Qualification, abuse and consent controls

The rules behind runbook steps 4 to 7. Specify every line that applies; a line you leave out is a decision, so say so.

## Where and when to ask

- At a moment of success: right after a first result is delivered, after a 9 or 10 satisfaction score, after a milestone the user cares about.
- Never during setup or after an error.
- One prompt per moment, easy to dismiss, not shown again for 30 days after a dismissal.

## Qualifying a reward

- Pay on a real action by the invitee (their first meaningful use), never on signup alone.
- Hold the reward for 48 hours to 14 days, against refunds and chargebacks.
- Cap rewards per referrer per month.
- Show the referrer a pending state, so a held reward is not read as a missing one.

## Abuse controls

- No reward when the invitee shares the referrer's device, payment method or workspace domain.
- Rate limits on invitations sent.
- A manual review queue above a threshold (name the threshold).
- A pause switch for rewards, decided by the lead: pause, grant nothing silently, tell affected users the reason, review what is pending.
- Employees and partners are excluded from K and from cost.

## Consent

- Never access a user's contacts, scrape addresses or send an invitation on someone's behalf without a clear, specific action by that user.
- The invitee can opt out of reminders at once.
- Personal-data and marketing-consent questions go to Defne. Do not guess the law of any country.
