# Templates

Copy a block and fill every line. A line you cannot fill is a gap: name it and who can close it.

## A finding

```text
F<n> | S<1-4> | <step, element, device or segment>
Evidence: <count, recording, screenshot path or element read>
Principle: <clarity | effort | anxiety | relevance | distraction>
Fix: <the change, specific enough to build>   Moves: <metric>   Owner: <Jamileh | Deniz | Selin | test>
```

## The funnel table (one row per step, all counts from the same 28 days)

```text
| Step | Visitors | Step conversion | Cumulative | Mobile | Desktop | Paid | Organic | New | Returning |
```

Step conversion is visitors at step n over step n-1; cumulative is over the first step. Fill the segment columns with the step conversion of that segment, or write "unknown" and list the instrumentation as a gap.

## The report, in the order of the role contract

```text
1. Executive summary: the leak, who carries it, the top three actions
2. Funnel table and the segment split
3. Findings (the block above), S1 first
4. Backlog: ICE-scored, S1 and S2 marked "fix", the rest "test"
5. Briefs for the top three experiments, written with ab-test-setup
6. Which flows were walked, at which widths, and which were not
7. Gaps: the data and access still missing, with an owner
```
