# Templates

Copy a block and fill every line. A line you cannot fill is a gap: name it and who can close it.

## The top of the brief

```text
GOAL        <one event, for example "creates a first project">
SEGMENT     <who is in, and what they have already done>
EXIT        <goal event | unsubscribe | reply with a question | purchase>   evaluated at send time, not only at entry
WINDOW      earliest <date>, latest <date>
FACTS       <each product fact and proof point, with its source>
```

## One email

```text
EMAIL <n>    day <d>    purpose <start | proof | objection | help | decision>
SUBJECT      <40 to 50 characters, the reason in plain words>
PREVIEW      <the second half of the subject, not a repeat>
BODY         <one idea, short paragraphs, who is sending and why the person gets it>
CTA          <verb + outcome>   LINK <url>?utm_source=email&utm_medium=lifecycle&utm_campaign=<campaign>&utm_content=e<n>
EXIT RULE    <skip or stop when ...>
```

## The sequence table

```text
| # | Day | Purpose | Subject | Preview | CTA |
```

One purpose per row and no repeats. The last email of a time-limited trial is tied to the real end date.
