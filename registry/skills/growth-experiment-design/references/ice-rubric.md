# ICE rubric

Each criterion is scored 1 to 10; **ICE is the mean of Impact, Confidence and Ease**. Score each number from the band that fits and write the reason next to it in the backlog row, so a reader can disagree with the reason and not only with the number.

## Impact: how far the bottleneck stage moves

| Score | The change is expected to move the bottleneck stage by |
|---|---|
| 9 to 10 | 20 percent relative or more |
| 6 to 8 | 10 to 20 percent relative |
| 3 to 5 | under 10 percent relative |
| 1 to 2 | only a vanity metric |

Relative means against the stage's own conversion: 25 to 30 percent is 20 percent relative (5 points on a base of 25).

## Confidence: how good the evidence is

| Score | The hypothesis rests on |
|---|---|
| 9 to 10 | an earlier test on this product |
| 7 to 8 | numbers from this funnel |
| 4 to 6 | qualitative evidence: session recordings, support tickets, interviews |
| 1 to 3 | opinion |

A hypothesis with no evidence clause is a wish: Confidence 1 to 3.

## Ease: how much work it is to ship

| Score | Effort |
|---|---|
| 10 | copy or configuration, by one person, in a day |
| 7 | a front-end change in one to three days |
| 4 | needs backend or data work |
| 1 | more than a month |

Scores between the anchors (5, 6, 8, 9) are used for work that sits between two of these descriptions.

An experiment that needs more than 14 days of traffic at this page's volume is not scored: it is not in the backlog (runbook step 4).
