# Freeze and pass Sound to production

Use the sibling [media contract](../../audiovisual-review/references/media-contract.md) for executable fields; do not create a second timeline schema. Direction annotations and approval records live alongside it, not as extra canonical JSON keys. A source checkout or installation must include that sibling resource; discovery of this Skill alone does not establish a complete installed media workflow.

Before freeze, Motion owns the coordinated film timeline: contiguous scene ranges, canvas, integer fps and totalFrames. For standalone audio, Sound owns the Lead-approved cue/timing record and uses that same complete canonical schema when helpers require it; its scene/canvas fields are processing records, not a requirement for visuals, a storyboard or a renderer. Sound proposes applicable `vo`, `music` and `sfx` ranges, with Kaan-approved exact text and voice consent for included speech. A wordless cue needs no VO, invented narration or Kaan approval: mark speech, transcript, captions, pronunciation and voice-consent records not applicable with a reason, retaining all source-rights requirements. Express every interval as `[startFrame,endFrame)`. At 60fps, frame 120 starts at 2 seconds and `[120,300)` lasts 3 seconds. At 48kHz those boundaries map to 96,000 and 240,000 sample frames. For other valid fps, round each boundary according to the actual processor and inspect alignment; do not assume every frame is an integer number of samples.

The approval record names timeline revision, canonical fingerprint, copy/brand versions, scratch/final status, route and reviewer. The fingerprint covers revision, dimensions, scenes, cues and speech text. A new word, cue timing or canvas revision requires revalidation and a newly issued handoff. Never paste the old hash into a changed document. Fingerprints detect change; they do not establish human approval.

## Producer hand-off

Send audio-generation the palette, source/rights plan, exact script/pronunciation and cue duration requirements. Send audio-post-production source/take IDs, original files/hashes, trim intentions, gain/ducking plan and destination loudness preset. The finishing output is a stereo 48kHz WAV master; full-duration aligned pre-master cue stems for included VO/music/SFX; canonical manifest; frame cue sheet; applicable transcript/captions; provenance; measured and listening review records.

Stems retain relative mix gains. Their sum reconstructs the pre-master where that is the declared stem policy; it need not reproduce nonlinear mastering. State whether music ducking is printed in a stem or held as editable automation. Do not normalize every stem to master loudness or trim away its frame-zero alignment.

For film, Motion integrates only the approved audio revision/fingerprint in either renderer, remeasures the muxed output and reviews sync/tails/captions. Sound reviews the final presentation audio too; an approved WAV does not prove its AAC transcode. Standalone WAV/sting delivery goes directly to Chris through required applicable audio/provenance review, with no Motion, renderer, video or mux prerequisite. Chris receives separate numerical acceptance, listening findings, rights disposition and any blocked placement. Required missing checks remain **unverified** and block acceptance; mark an irrelevant check not applicable with a reason. A successful manifest check reports declared values and path/schema consistency, not actual decoded duration or human quality.
