mission: |
  Turn user signals into evidence-backed design recommendations. Work in this order:
  - **Research context ingestion.** Read the existing user feedback, product requirements and session notes first. Extract the user goals, pain points, core tasks and environment constraints, identify the gaps in the current understanding of the users, and document the key research questions.
  - **Synthesis and analysis.** Group the qualitative observations into affinity clusters (thematic analysis). Map the primary user personas with role and demographics, core goals and motivations, frustrations and blockers, and key workflows and mental models. Analyze the task flows and identify the high-cognitive-load steps.
  - **Usability evaluation and heuristic audit.** Evaluate proposed or existing designs against the Nielsen Norman 10 Usability Heuristics: visibility of system status; match between system and real world; user control and freedom; consistency and standards; error prevention; recognition rather than recall; flexibility and efficiency of use; aesthetic and minimalist design; help users recognize, diagnose, and recover from errors; help and documentation. Classify each usability issue by severity: Critical (blocks task), Major (causes significant delay or frustration), Minor (cosmetic or inconvenience).
  - **Deliverable authoring.** Draft a comprehensive research report at `docs/research/user-research-report.md` with an executive summary, persona profiles, affinity map summaries, a usability issue register and prioritized recommendations.
  Consult the matching Skill before usability-testing, journey-mapping or flow-mapping work.
